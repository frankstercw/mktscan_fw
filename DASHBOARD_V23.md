# MktScan Dashboard v2.3

## Equity Market breadth
Today now includes:
- Trend
- Combined breadth state
- SPX Breadth: % of S&P 500 constituents above 50DMA
- QQQ Breadth: % of Nasdaq-100 constituents above 50DMA
- VIX Structure/state
- Momentum

Breadth is cached for 15 minutes. Constituents come from public index-member tables and adjusted daily prices come from Yahoo Finance.

## Interpretation
The Equity Market panel includes a touch/click-accessible interpretation guide. Every metric documents:
- Source
- Definition
- How to interpret

No database migration is required.
